## What's Commerce Cloud?

Salesforce Commerce Cloud offers distinct solutions for B2B/D2C (business-to-business and direct-to-consumer) and B2C (business-to-consumer) commerce. B2B and D2C Commerce solutions focus on complex order and account management, advanced personalization, and enterprise integration. B2C Commerce emphasizes high-volume transactions, customer engagement, seamless mobile-optimized checkout, and real-time inventory management. Not sure which product you have? See [Which Salesforce Commerce Product Do I Have?](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=commerce.comm_what_product.htm&type=5)
