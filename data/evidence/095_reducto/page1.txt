Reducto measures usage separately for each product. Your contract and rate card is the source of truth.
Contracted customers may have negotiated rates that differ from the list prices below.
These listed prices go into effect on September 1, 2026. You can view the
migration guide
and our
legacy pricing chart
.

Batch processing
Parse (r-1 and r-1 mini) and Extract jobs submitted through the batch queue receive a 20% usage discount. Deep Extract is not eligible for the batch queue or its discount. Batch jobs run asynchronously with a 12-hour completion guarantee. See
Batch Queue
.

Monitoring usage
Keeping track of how you’re using Reducto is easy. You can:
View usage over time in the
Reducto Studio
usage dashboard.
Inspect the per-product breakdown for individual jobs.
Configure usage alerts, either to notify with a dollar amount or credit amount threshold.
Export usage programmatically with the
Usage Export API
.
