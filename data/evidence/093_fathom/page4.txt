Authentication
To connect to our API, you must use the
Authorization
header with a bearer token. This can be obtained from your dashboard, under Settings → API. Your API key has complete access to your account (depending on the permissions you set), so please protect it.
You should also send
Accept: application/json
so that errors are returned as JSON.
