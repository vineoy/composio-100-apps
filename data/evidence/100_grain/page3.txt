Grain MCP
Connect Grain to Claude or ChatGPT and query your meeting data with natural language.
Updated in the last 15 minutes
Table of contents
Grain's MCP (Model Context Protocol) server gives AI assistants direct access to your meetings, transcripts, AI notes, and workspace data. Ask questions, generate reports, create clips, and organize recordings, all from your AI chat interface.
For technical setup and the full tool reference, see the
Grain MCP developer docs
.
​
Set up with Claude
Step 1: Add the
Claude Connector
integration
In Claude, go to ’Customize’ → ‘Connectors’
Click '+' to search for connectors
Search for ‘Grain’
Select & connect Grain
You’ll be redirected to Grain to approve the connection
Step 2: Verify
From a new Claude chat, send this test prompt:
Tell me about my most recent Grain meeting
If the MCP doesn’t work as expected, click '+' and confirm 'Grain' appears under 'Connectors' before trying again.
Set up with ChatGPT
Step 1: Add the
ChatGPT App
integration
Go to 'Apps' and search for 'Grain'
Select 'Grain' and click 'Connect'
Sign in and approve ChatGPT access to your Grain account data
Step 2: Verify
From a new ChatGPT chat, send the following test prompt:
Tell me about my most recent Grain meeting
If the MCP doesn’t work properly, click '+' and select Grain as a source before trying again.

FAQ
Is my data safe?
Grain's MCP server uses OAuth and HTTPS. Developers can also connect with a personal access token; see the
developer docs
. Your data stays in your Grain account and is processed temporarily by AI to answer your questions. View your connected AI client's privacy policy for more information about data handling.
What data can the MCP access?
Only data you already have access to in Grain — meetings you attended, contacts, AI notes, transcripts, and deal info (with HubSpot connected). It cannot access meetings you weren't part of.
Can I control which capabilities my AI client uses?
Yes. In Claude’s ‘Connectors’ menu you can toggle individual Grain tools on or off. ChatGPT offers a similar feature, allowing you to specify when ChatGPT should ask for permission to use an MCP tool.
