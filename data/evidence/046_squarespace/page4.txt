Connect a Client
The Squarespace MCP server is a remote server: your client connects to it over HTTPS, and there's nothing to install. In Gemini, Squarespace is already built in. For Claude, ChatGPT, and other clients, you add it with the server URL.
Gemini
Squarespace is built into Gemini as one of its
connected apps
, so there's no server URL to enter.
On
gemini.google.com
, open
Customize
in the sidebar, then
Connectors
.
Find
Squarespace
and click
Add
.
In a chat, type
@Squarespace
or just ask for a domain.
Gemini offers Squarespace to personal Google accounts in the US, in English, on the web, mobile, and Windows apps. Only the
Domains
tools are available in Gemini.
Server URL
Code
https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp
Use this URL in Claude, ChatGPT, and any other MCP client. Menu names in each client change between releases, so the steps below may not match your version exactly.

Any other MCP client
Configure a remote MCP server with:
URL:
https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp
Transport:
Streamable HTTP
Only the
Domains
tools are available in other clients. The Commerce tools are currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT.
Sign in to Squarespace
The
Domains
tools work without an account. The
Commerce
tools need you to sign in with the Squarespace account you use to manage your websites, and are currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT.
When your client offers to connect or sign in, you're taken to the standard Squarespace login page. After you sign in, your client receives access to the signed-in tools and they appear in its tool list. Until then, only the Domains tools are listed.
Signing in doesn't give the assistant more access than you have. Every tool acts as you, with the permissions you have on the website it's working on. To disconnect, remove or sign out of the connector in your client's settings.
For client developers: sign-in uses OAuth 2.1. The authorization server is advertised in the server's protected resource metadata at
https://mcp.squarespace.com/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp
.
Verify your connection
After connecting, try:
"Is
example-test-domain.com
available on Squarespace?"
The assistant should check the domain and reply with availability and pricing.
If you've signed in, also try:
"Show me my five most recent orders."
The assistant should reply with orders from your store. If it says it has no Squarespace tools for this, check that you completed sign-in and that the connector is turned on for the chat.
