Frequently asked questions
What can I sell on Gumroad?
Digital products, e-books, courses, tutorials, and memberships—almost anything! Creators in just about every industry use (and love) Gumroad, from digital artists, writers, musicians and other creative-types to business-minded entrepreneurs and tech gurus like our SaaS creators. A better question is, "What can't I sell on Gumroad?"
What can't I sell on Gumroad?
Outside of physical goods, things like illegal or illicit goods or services, securities & financial instruments, credit repair, gambling & lotteries, harassment & extremist content, copyright infringement & piracy, or obscene or adult content are not permitted on Gumroad. A full list can be found
here
.
How do I get paid?
Gumroad's goal is to help creators make a living doing what they love, so we make it as simple as possible to get paid. How we pay creators, whether through direct deposit or PayPal, varies by country.
What are the fees?
Unlike other platforms, Gumroad doesn't charge you a monthly fee. Instead, our fees are deducted as a small percentage of every sale, so we only make money when you do.
Can I use Gumroad for memberships?
Yes!
Whether it's for a podcast, online course, or SaaS subscriptions, Gumroad makes it easy to manage memberships.
How does Gumroad handle taxes?
Gumroad is your merchant of record, which means we automatically handle all sales tax collection and remittance worldwide. You don't need to manage any tax settings or applications - we take care of everything.
What do I need to do about taxes?
Nothing! The change took effect on January 1, 2025, and our Terms of Service reflect it. You can continue selling as usual.
What about international sales?
We'll handle all tax obligations for your global sales automatically. You don't need to worry about VAT, GST, or any other international tax requirements.
What happens to my tax collection settings?
All tax settings will be automatically disabled in your dashboard. This includes both the state tax settings in your creator profile and any product-specific tax settings. You won't need to manage these anymore since we'll handle all tax collection automatically.
Will Gumroad collect taxes everywhere?
We collect taxes in regions where we have tax obligations as a merchant of record. This varies by location, and we automatically handle the appropriate tax collection for each sale.
Need a Reseller Certificate from Gumroad?
Some creators may have an obligation to file sales tax (or other indirect tax) returns already, and will need to report their sales on Gumroad as "sales to other retailers for purposes of resale", which are not taxable. To justify this treatment to your tax authority, you will need to download and save
this Reseller Certificate
.
