How to Setup and Use the HighLevel MCP Server
Modified on: Sun, 15 Nov, 2026 at  2:10 AM
Connect your AI assistant to HighLevel so it can find CRM records and perform supported actions from your instructions. The HighLevel MCP Server, also called the LeadConnector MCP Server, connects through OAuth: sign in, choose the sub-account or sub-accounts to include, and approve the requested permissions.
This guide covers setup for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Muse, and Grok Bot, along with how to work across authorized sub-accounts and discover available operations.

Technical Reference
What is the HighLevel MCP Server?
MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. It gives AI assistants a standard way to discover and use tools from connected applications. With the HighLevel MCP Server, your assistant can work with supported CRM data and actions without you needing to know the underlying API.
The active catalog includes
more than 600 read, create, update, and delete operations across 38 domains
. The operations available to your connection depend on the OAuth scopes and permissions you approve.
Before You Connect
You need access to the HighLevel sub-account or sub-accounts you want to connect and a supported AI client. ChatGPT users can find the official HighLevel plugin in Plugins. Muse and Grok Bot also offer officially listed apps. For custom MCP connections, use the corresponding URL below.

Confirm Your Connection
Start with a simple request:
Find the last 5 contacts I added in HighLevel.
Your assistant can discover the available contact operations and retrieve records within the permissions you granted.
For an agency connection, name the sub-account you want to use.
Working with One or Multiple Sub-accounts
Single sub-account
A single-sub-account connection is bound to the sub-account you choose during OAuth authorization for the lifetime of that connection.
Agency connection
Where agency-wide access is available, you can connect once and work across the sub-accounts selected during installation.
Choose the sub-accounts during installation.
Select one, several, or all of the sub-accounts offered by the agency installation.
Name the sub-account in your request.
If the intended sub-account is unclear, the assistant uses
list_locations
and asks you to choose.
Switch within the same chat.
Ask about another authorized sub-account without reconnecting.
For example:
List the contacts tagged “vip-2026” in Downtown Clinic, then compare the count with Uptown Med Spa.
The assistant resolves the sub-account names, queries each sub-account separately, and compares the results.
Every request targets one sub-account
and is authorized against your installation. The connection cannot access sub-accounts you did not include.
Availability:
Single-sub-account connections are available today. Agency-wide connections are rolling out through the multi-sub-account, or bulk, installation option. If installation offers only one sub-account, the multi-sub-account option is not enabled for your app yet.
Watch the multiple-sub-account walkthrough

How the Four MCP Tools Work
The server exposes four unified tools. Your assistant uses these tools to find and run the operations available to your connection.
Tool
Purpose
list_locations
Find the sub-accounts available to the connection, or return its single bound sub-account.
search_operations
Discover active operations for the task, including record searches and read, create, update, and delete actions.
describe_operation
Check an operation's inputs, example payload, required scopes, and safety information.
execute_operation
Run an operation within OAuth scopes, permissions, sub-account boundaries, and built-in safety checks.
You can ask your assistant to use
search_operations
to check what is available to your connection at any time.

Example: complete a task from one prompt
After connecting, you can give your assistant a request such as:
Find the contact with email
jane@example.com
, add the tag “vip-2026”, and create an opportunity for them in “Sales Pipeline” worth $5,000.
The assistant uses
search_operations
to discover the contact lookup, tagging, and opportunity operations. It checks their inputs, scopes, and safety information with
describe_operation
, then uses
execute_operation
to find the contact and perform the supported actions. It reports the results back in natural language.
Permissions and Security
You select the sub-accounts.
Each request is authorized against the installation and can access only a sub-account included in that connection.
OAuth scopes limit actions.
The assistant can perform only the operations allowed by your grant.
Sensitive actions have additional checks.
Sensitive and irreversible operations are subject to confirmation and safety checks before execution.
You control ongoing access.
Review or revoke the connection's access from your LeadConnector account.
Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Do I need a Private Integration Token?
The client connections in this guide use OAuth. Complete the LeadConnector sign-in and consent flow to authorize access; you do not need to manually create or rotate a token for these connections.
Q: Which server URL should I use?
The HighLevel plugin in ChatGPT does not require manually entering an MCP server URL. For custom MCP connections, use the URL listed for your client in the connection table above. Claude clients use the Anthropic endpoint, ChatGPT and Codex use the OpenAI endpoint, and Cursor uses its own endpoint. Muse and Grok Bot also offer officially listed apps.
Q: Why do I see four tools instead of hundreds?
The four tools let the assistant discover and run the active operation catalog. Individual CRM operations are found through
search_operations
, inspected through
describe_operation
, and run through
execute_operation
.
Q: How many operations are available?
The active catalog includes more than 600 operations across 38 domains. Your connection can use only the operations allowed by its OAuth scopes and permissions. Ask your assistant to search for the operation you need.
Q: Can my assistant create, update, or delete records?
Yes, where the operation is supported and your OAuth grant permits it. Sensitive and irreversible actions also have confirmation and safety checks.
Q: Can I use one connection for several sub-accounts?
Agencies can use a multi-sub-account installation where it is enabled. Each operation still runs against one authorized sub-account. If installation only offers a single sub-account, the multi-sub-account option is not yet enabled for your app.
Q: Can the assistant access a sub-account I did not select?
No. Requests outside the sub-accounts included in the installation are refused.
Q: How do I remove access?
Review or revoke the integration's access from your LeadConnector account.
