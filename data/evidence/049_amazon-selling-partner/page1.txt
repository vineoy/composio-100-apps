Important Update
Cancellation of Third-Party Developer Selling Partner API (SP-API) Fees
Our developer community is incredibly important to us. We previously announced changes to developer fees related to SP-API usage, and we value your feedback on those planned changes. After careful consideration, we have decided that we will not move forward with the SP-API usage and annual fees at this time. Supporting your ability to innovate, build and grow is a top priority and we appreciate partnering together to provide high quality solutions for Amazon sellers.
In the coming weeks, we will be making updates to the Solution Provider Portal (SPP) to reflect this decision, such as removing the fee preview dashboard. If you previously provided a credit card, you can delete it now through
this link in SPP
.
We are committed to supporting you as you build the next generation of solutions for selling partners.
Thank you for being an important part of the Amazon Selling Partner developer community.
