Smartsheet MCP Server overview
The Smartsheet Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server enables you to manage projects and Smartsheet data from within Smart Assist and AI assistants, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Gemini Enterprise Plus, Cursor, and others.
USM Content
Smart Assist
requires a Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Advanced Work Management plan.
MCP-enabled AI tools
require a Business, Enterprise, or Advanced Work Management plan.
When you're in Smartsheet using Smart Assist or working in an external AI tool (AI tool) connected to the Smartsheet MCP Server, you can use natural language to automate tasks, manage project data, and collaborate more efficiently.
The
Model Context Protocol (MCP)
is an open standard that enables AI systems to safely connect to external data sources and tools. It’s like a secure bridge between AI and your business applications–in this case, Smartsheet. Instead of manually copying data between Smartsheet and AI or performing repetitive tasks, MCP allows AI to interact directly with Smartsheet.
