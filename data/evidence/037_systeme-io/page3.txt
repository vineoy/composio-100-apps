Frequently asked questions about systeme.io pricing
How does systeme.io pricing work and what do I get on each plan?
Systeme.io has four plans based primarily on contact limits. Free: 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 course. Startup ($17/month): 5,000 contacts, 10 sales funnels, 5 courses. Webinar ($47/month): 10,000 contacts, 50 sales funnels, 20 courses, plus evergreen webinars. Unlimited ($97/month): unlimited contacts, funnels, courses and automation rules. All plans include unlimited email sends, unlimited sub-accounts, 0% transaction fees, and the same set of core tools.
Does systeme.io have a free plan and what are its limits?
Yes, the free plan supports up to 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 blog, 1 course, 1 affiliate program, and 1 custom domain. Emails sent are unlimited. The free plan never expires and does not require a credit card. Other features have lower limits on the free plan, like 1 automation rule, 1 email campaign and 500 students. If you need more, the Startup plan at $17/month is the next step.
Which systeme.io pricing plan should I choose for my business size?
Under 2,000 contacts: use the free plan. It costs nothing and covers most needs. 2,000 to 5,000 contacts: Startup at $17/month. 5,000 to 10,000 contacts: Webinar at $47/month (also adds evergreen webinars). Over 10,000 contacts: Unlimited at $97/month.
Is systeme.io cheaper than using separate tools for funnels, email, and courses?
Typically, yes, and by a lot. A comparable tool stack would include ClickFunnels ($97/month) for funnels, Mailchimp ($20/month) for email, Teachable ($39/month) for courses, and Tapfiliate ($89/month) for affiliates. That’s $245/month before connecting them. systeme.io covers all of this on the free plan or for $17/month. Even the Unlimited plan at $97/month costs the same as ClickFunnels alone.
Can I change or cancel my systeme.io plan at any time and what happens to my data?
Yes. You can upgrade your plan or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. If you cancel, you keep your current plan until your next billing date, then your account reverts to the free plan. To downgrade, you have two options:
contact our support team
to convert the remaining days of your current plan into the lower plan, or cancel your current plan, wait until your account reverts to the free plan on your next billing date, then upgrade to the plan you want
